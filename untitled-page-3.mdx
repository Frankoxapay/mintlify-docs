---
title: "Generate Invoice"
description: "Create a hosted OxaPay payment page and redirect your customer to complete a crypto payment."
openapi: "/openapi/oxapay-generate-invoice-openapi.yaml POST /v1/payment/invoice"
---

Use **Generate Invoice** when you want OxaPay to host the checkout experience. Your backend creates the invoice, OxaPay returns a `payment_url`, and the payer opens that URL to choose a supported cryptocurrency and complete the payment.

<Note>
  If you need to keep the payment UI inside your own application and receive the payment address, amount, currency, network, and QR details directly, use [Generate White Label](/api-reference/payment/generate-white-label) instead.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Supported assets, fiat currencies, and networks can change over time.
